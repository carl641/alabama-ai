Is an AI assessment right for you?

Consulting makes sense when you know AI could help but not where to start, or when you've tried a few tools and nothing stuck.

Good fit

  • Repetitive admin, intake or follow-up work that takes hours each week
  • Several AI ideas and no way to rank them
  • Leadership wants a plan before approving spend
  • You want an independent view on vendor pitches

Probably not yet

  • You already know the exact workflow to automate: go straight to a build
  • Core processes aren't written down or change weekly
  • You're looking for a general AI strategy deck with no follow-through
Deliverables

What you receive

Everything is written for owners and managers, not engineers, and it's yours to keep whether or not you hire us to build.

Workflow baseline

For the processes we review: steps, people, systems, volumes and time spent. This is the "before" measurement any future project is judged against.

Prioritized opportunity list

Each candidate scored for business value, implementation effort, data readiness and risk, so the order is defensible rather than exciting.

Build-versus-buy recommendation

For each top opportunity: use a feature you already pay for, buy a product, configure an automation platform, or commission a custom build.

Scoped first pilot

A single pilot with inputs, outputs, human review points, success measures, timeline and cost, ready to approve or take to another provider.

Risk and data notes

What data each idea touches, who needs access, and where human approval is required before anything reaches a customer.

Read-out session

A working session with your leadership to walk through findings and agree next steps.

Questions about AI consulting

Do we have to hire you to build what the assessment recommends?

No. The roadmap and pilot scope are written so any competent provider, or your own team, can act on them.

How much of our time does it take?

Mostly short interviews with the people who do the work, plus a kick-off and a read-out with decision-makers. We schedule around operations.

Do you need access to our systems?

Usually read-only views or screen-shares are enough for an assessment. We don't need passwords to sensitive systems at this stage. See our security approach.
