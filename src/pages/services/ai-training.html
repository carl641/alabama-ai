Who AI training is for

Training helps when the tools are already available, or about to be, and the gap is knowing how to use them well. It's a people investment rather than a software build, and often the fastest, lowest-cost first step with AI.

Good fit

  • You pay for AI tools that few people use
  • Staff use AI informally and you want consistent, safe habits
  • Leadership wants to understand AI before approving projects
  • You need a written AI usage policy your team will actually follow

Probably not a fit

  • You want a process fully automated: see workflow automation
  • You're looking for a general "future of AI" keynote
  • Staff have no access to AI tools and none are planned
  • You need technical training for software developers building AI systems

Measuring adoption

How we know it worked

Attendance isn't the goal. We agree adoption measures with you up front, such as:

  • How many people use AI for at least one task each week, a few weeks after training
  • Which tasks from the prompt library are in regular use
  • Staff confidence and understanding of the data rules, before and after
  • Questions and problems raised in follow-up, and whether they got resolved

We'll report what we find honestly, including where adoption is low and why.

Questions about AI training

Do staff need any technical background?

No. Sessions are designed for people who write emails, handle customers and use spreadsheets, not programmers.

Which AI tools should we use?

If you already pay for a tool, we usually train on that. If not, we'll talk through options based on your data rules and budget. We don't take commissions for recommending tools. {{todo: Confirm there are no vendor referral or reseller arrangements, or disclose them.}}

Can you help us write an AI policy?

We provide draft usage guidelines as part of training. Anything with legal or HR implications should be reviewed by your own advisers before adoption.

Will one workshop be enough?

For small teams with one main tool, sometimes. Habits usually need a follow-up session to stick, which is why we build one in.
