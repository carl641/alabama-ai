What an AI voice agent does

A voice agent is software that holds a spoken phone conversation. It listens, works out what the caller needs, asks follow-up questions and takes an action: booking a slot, logging the request, sending a text confirmation or transferring the call.

It works from rules you approve: which call types it handles, what it must never promise, and exactly when a person takes over. It is not a replacement for your front desk on complicated or sensitive calls.

Good fit

  • Calls go to voicemail after hours or while staff are on jobs
  • Most calls are routine: booking, hours, service area, status
  • You have clear rules for what counts as urgent
  • You use a calendar or scheduling tool the agent can book into

Probably not a fit

  • Most calls are complex negotiations or sensitive conversations
  • Calls routinely involve health, legal or financial details that need specialist review before any automation
  • You can't provide an on-call person or fallback for emergencies
  • Most inquiries come by web chat: consider an AI chatbot
Call scenarios

New service requests

Collect the caller's name, address, problem and preferred time, then book it or log it for your team to confirm.

Appointment booking & changes

Offer open slots from your calendar, confirm by text, and handle simple reschedules.

Routine questions

Hours, service area, what to bring, how to pay, drawn from answers you've approved.

Urgent calls

Recognize the signs you've defined as urgent and transfer the call or alert your on-call person immediately.

Pick up when every line is busy so callers aren't left on hold or sent to voicemail.

Message taking

For anything outside its scope, take a clear message and send your team a written summary.

Consent, recording & regulated use

Rules to settle before launch

Phone calls come with legal and ethical obligations. Before any voice agent goes live, you and your advisers should settle:

  • Disclosure: callers should know they're speaking to an automated assistant. Our designs say so at the start of the call.
  • Recording & transcription: whether calls are recorded or transcribed, and what notice or consent that requires for your callers.
  • Outbound calls and texts: automated outbound calling and texting have their own consent rules; we treat these as a separate review.
  • Regulated information: calls involving health, financial or legal details may carry extra obligations.

We are not lawyers and don't give legal advice. We build to the requirements you and qualified advisers set, and we'll flag questions for them as we design.

Testing & failure handling

What happens when the AI gets it wrong

  • We run scripted test calls covering every call type, plus callers who mumble, interrupt, change their mind or go off topic
  • If the agent is unsure, it takes a message or transfers rather than guessing
  • If the phone, calendar or AI service fails, calls fall back to voicemail or a person, never to silence
  • You review transcripts or summaries during the first weeks, and we adjust scripts from what you see
  • It never promises prices, diagnoses or commitments you haven't approved

Known limitations: voice agents can mishear names, addresses and accents, struggle on bad connections, and handle emotional callers less well than a person. We design confirmations and handoffs around those limits.

Questions about AI receptionists

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. We design agents to disclose that they're automated at the start of the call and to offer a way to reach a person. Hiding it damages trust and may create legal risk.

What if a caller has an emergency?

You define what counts as urgent. When the agent hears those signs it transfers the call or alerts your on-call person right away. If it can't reach anyone, it tells the caller what to do next using wording you've approved.

Can it keep our existing phone number?

Usually, by forwarding calls to the agent at set times or when lines are busy. The exact setup depends on your phone provider.

Are calls recorded?

That's your decision, made with your advisers. Recording and transcription can help quality review but come with notice and consent obligations that vary by situation.

Can it make outbound calls?

This page covers inbound calls. Automated outbound calls and texts have separate consent rules and need their own review before we'd build them.
