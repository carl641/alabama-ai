Editorial standards

How these guides are written

We sell AI services, so you should know how we keep these guides useful rather than promotional.

  • Each guide answers one buyer decision and gives a direct answer near the top.
  • We don't publish market statistics we can't source. Numbers used to explain an idea are labeled as illustrative or hypothetical.
  • Every guide includes a section on when not to buy.
  • No rankings of agencies, including rankings that happen to put us first.
  • Each guide names its author and reviewer and shows when it was last updated.
