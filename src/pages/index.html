Questions

Straight answers

Do we need to be a tech company to use AI?

No. Most useful projects automate ordinary work: answering calls, following up on leads, moving information between the tools you already use. We handle the technical setup and train your team on the parts they touch.

Will AI replace our staff?

That's not how we design systems. We target repetitive tasks and build in human review for anything that affects customers, money or commitments. The usual goal is giving the people you have more time for work that needs judgment.

What does a first project cost?

It depends on scope, which is why we start with a fixed-price assessment or pilot. Our pricing page explains the options, and our guide on what an AI agency costs explains the factors behind them.

What happens to our data?

We work with the minimum access needed, prefer your own accounts, and document which vendors process what. The details are on our security page.

What if AI isn't the right answer?

Then we'll tell you. Sometimes a simple automation rule, a better form or an existing software feature beats a custom AI build. See AI vs. traditional automation.
