Questions about location

Do we need to be near you to work together?

No. Build, testing and support are done remotely in your own software accounts. Location matters mainly if you want in-person workshops or an on-site workflow review.

Is remote AI implementation as effective as on-site?

For the build itself, yes: the work happens in software either way. Where in-person time helps is understanding how your team really works and training people who prefer hands-on sessions. We'll recommend in-person steps only where they're worth the travel.
