Worked example

Illustrative example: a 60-person engineering services firm

Illustrative example with a fictional firm. It shows a typical workflow, not a client result.

A fictional engineering services firm near Research Park is hiring for a dozen roles. Its one HR coordinator spends most days reading applications, emailing candidates to find interview times, and answering the same onboarding questions from new hires.

A scoped pilot changes three things. Applications are summarized into a one-page format against the requirements the hiring manager wrote, with no automated ranking or rejection. Candidates pick interview times from managers' real availability. New hires get answers to routine questions (parking, benefits enrollment dates, first-week schedule) from an assistant trained only on the approved onboarding handbook.

Before any of it goes live, the firm's IT and security lead reviews which services are used, what data each one sees, and where it's stored. Nothing in the pilot touches program or technical data.
