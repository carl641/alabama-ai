Software & usage fees

What you'll pay vendors directly

Most AI systems depend on software you'll pay for monthly or by usage: an AI model provider, an automation platform, a phone or texting service, a chatbot platform, sometimes a database or hosting. We set these up in your business's accounts, billed to you, so you can see the costs and keep the system if we part ways.

Usage-based costs rise and fall with volume: more calls, messages or documents means a higher bill. Vendors also change their pricing and models from time to time.

What your estimate shows

  • Each vendor the system relies on, and what it's used for
  • The vendor's pricing basis (per user, per minute, per message, per task)
  • An estimated monthly range at your expected volumes, with the assumptions written down
  • How to set spending limits or alerts where the vendor supports them

Estimating rules

How we make an estimate

We don't price by guessing what you'll pay. Estimates are built from the scope, using the same rules every time, and the written estimate shows the working.

  1. Count the pieces

    Number of workflows, systems to connect, channels (web, phone, text, email) and user groups. Each connection adds build and test time.

  2. Assess the unknowns

    Messy data, undocumented processes or software without a good way to connect raise the estimate, or become a paid discovery step first.

  3. Size the testing

    Customer-facing and higher-risk workflows need more test cases, more review rounds and stricter approvals.

  4. Add documentation & training

    Handover time is estimated, not squeezed in at the end.

Pricing questions

Why start with an assessment or pilot instead of the full build?

Because it limits your risk. A small fixed-price step shows whether the idea works in your business, and it produces the information needed to price the larger build accurately.

Do you guarantee a return on investment?

No. We set measurable success criteria against a baseline and report honestly against them, but results depend on your volumes, adoption and many things outside our control.
